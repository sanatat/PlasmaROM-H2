# Research portfolio note — PlasmaROM-H2

**Appropriate title:** Independent computational kinetic-modelling and numerical-verification prototype (AI-assisted).

The repository demonstrates Python-based numerical ODE/PDE verification, conservative species transport, an analytically checked energy-balance surrogate, and prototype graph/energy channel screening. It also includes optional interfaces to the published Alzueta NH3/H2 chemistry mechanism and the Cantera 3.2 two-term EEDF solver on a sourced N2/O2 collision set. The optional physical-mechanism runs have **not** been executed in this offline build environment, and therefore are not claimed as completed results.

**Honest application language:** "I developed and tested a small Python research prototype for conservative reactive transport, numerical verification and kinetic graph screening; I also prepared research workflows for Cantera NH3/H2 chemistry and Boltzmann EEDF calculations. I am currently extending this toward validated state-to-state plasma kinetics."

Do not list the project as validated P-DRGEP, experimental plasma-assisted combustion, CFD DNS/LES, published mechanisms, or a completed high-performance solver.
